# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in tukey.

## Issues

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome as issues.

A good bug report says what you ran, what you expected, and what you got.

If it's an accuracy question, it helps to include the SciPy or MATLAB call you're comparing against.

## Pull requests

Please open an issue before a pull request.

tukey is maintained by one person, and its design and accuracy are checked closely,
I may not accept changes I haven't agreed first.

Small fixes, such as typos, are fine without an issue.

## Working on tukey

[docs/development.md](docs/development.md) explains how tukey is built and tested,
how reference vectors are made, and the conventions it follows.

[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) shows the planned and unplanned features.
